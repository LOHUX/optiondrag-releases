# Privacy

OptionDrag uses Accessibility and mouse events for window management. It does not require screen recording, account sign-in, a license server or telemetry. Update checks are opt-in and use the signed HTTPS feed; the host receives ordinary connection metadata. Automatic installation is disabled.

This repository, downloads and optional issue submissions are hosted by GitHub and follow GitHub’s policies. Issues are public; do not include sensitive data. Email is handled by your and LOHUX’s email providers. Optional payments open PayPal’s hosted page and follow PayPal’s policies; LOHUX’s website does not collect card details. You can download/use the app without paying or submitting feedback.

The public website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate traffic and performance, without analytics cookies or advertising identifiers. Collection excludes payment APIs, private receipts and Preview. The website respects Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control and offers an opt-out in its [Privacy page](https://optiondrag.app/privacy/). Traffic counts do not establish downloads or installations. GA4 and custom conversion tracking are not enabled.
